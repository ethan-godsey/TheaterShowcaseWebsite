# Ellie Evens site

A portfolio site for Ellie Evens, a musical theatre performer, with an admin she uses to keep it current herself.

## Language

**Hero**:
The top section of the site: her name, billing, lede, and the rotating headshots.
_Avoid_: Head section, header, banner

**Billing**:
The short line of disciplines under her name in the hero, e.g. "Soprano · Improv · Acting".
_Avoid_: tagline, subtitle

**Lede**:
The one- or two-line introduction under the billing in the hero.
_Avoid_: intro, blurb, description

**Headshot**:
A gallery photo she has marked to appear in the hero.
_Avoid_: hero image, portrait

**Paper colour**:
The site's background colour, from which the card, deep, and rule shades are derived.
_Avoid_: cream (that's only the default paper colour), background, theme
